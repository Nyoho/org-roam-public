* POV-Ray 3.7.0 RC6 - configure
これで configure の変なところが通った。
#+begin_src sh
g++  -o conftest -D_THREAD_SAFE -pthread -I/usr/local/include -L/usr/local/lib btest.cpp -lboost_thread-xgcc42-mt-d-1_50 -lboost_filesystem -lboost_system -D_THREAD_SAVE
#+end_src
from http://stackoverflow.com/questions/10079287/attempting-to-use-boost-filesystem-however-it-doesnt-seem-to-link

というわけでこれで configure (古い)
#+begin_src sh
./configure --with-libpng=/usr/local/lib --with-boost=/usr/local --with-boost-libdir=/usr/local/lib --with-boost-thread=boost_thread-xgcc42-mt-d-1_50 COMPILED_BY="Name <nobody@example.net>" LDFLAGS="-lboost_filesystem -lboost_system"
#+end_src

Gentoo の boost では
emerge boost libpng jpeg tiff
してから
#+begin_src sh
./configure --with-boost=$EPREFIX --with-boost-libdir=$EPREFIX/lib --with-boost-thread=boost_thread COMPILED_BY="Name <nobody@example.net>" LDFLAGS="-lboost_filesystem -lboost_system"
#+end_src
